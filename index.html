Welcome to GoodforMEdia's AI Conversation Cards

Created to spark meaningful and open dialogue about AI use with youth, this deck can be used as:

  • a game played between young people
  • a reflective activity between youth and parents or trusted adults
  • an icebreaker for youth classes, workshops, or group meetings

Have fun exploring your relationship with AI!

How to Play

  1. Separate the cards into three piles by category (Mild, Medium, Spicy), or shuffle the entire deck for a mix of categories.
  2. Choose a starting player — this can be done by flipping a coin.
  3. The starting player draws a card and reads the question aloud to the group.
  4. Each player answers the question from their own perspective. Encourage follow-up questions and discussion based on the answer.
  5. After a complete round of discussion, the next player draws a card and reads it aloud.
  6. Continue playing until everyone has had a chance to ask and answer.
  7. Feel free to make up your own way of playing the game!

Conversation Norms

Before talking about our experiences, it's important to make sure everyone starts off with shared expectations.

  • Be a good listener by treating everyone's stories and experiences with respect.
  • Approach these questions with curiosity, not judgment.
  • Take space, make space: say just enough.
  • If you are unsure about something, ask follow-up questions instead of assuming.

Card Types

Each card falls into one of three tiers.

  • Mild cards: relatively easy to answer — lighthearted, everyday conversation starters.
  • Medium cards: require more thought and can lead to discussion about personal experiences or opinions.
  • Spicy cards: designed to prompt deeper reflection and conversation.
